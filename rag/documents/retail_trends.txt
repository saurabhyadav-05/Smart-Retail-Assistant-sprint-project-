Retail sales are heavily influenced by seasonal demand, customer behavior, and economic conditions. Holiday periods such as Christmas and Black Friday usually lead to significant increases in weekly sales. Retail businesses often analyze customer purchasing trends to optimize inventory and improve profitability.

Temperature, fuel prices, and inflation can also impact customer shopping behavior. Higher inflation may reduce customer spending power, while lower fuel prices can increase store visits and transportation efficiency.

Modern retail systems use artificial intelligence and machine learning to predict future sales trends and improve decision making.
